### Management Assistant

The assistant may provide information and analysis covering:

- operations
- finance
- maintenance
- procurement
- compliance
- reporting
- forecasting
- cost
- profitability

### Equipment Diagnostics

The diagnostic system may analyze:

- fault codes
- alarms
- temperature
- pressure
- electrical information
- runtime
- fuel/electricity consumption
- vibration
- performance
- maintenance history
- previous failures
- spare parts
- operator observations
- photographs
- manuals and technical documents

AI recommendations require human review where the decision has
safety, financial, technical, or regulatory consequences.
